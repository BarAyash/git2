Tic-Tac-Toe

A two-player tic-tac-toe game that runs in the terminal. Players take turns placing X and O on a 3x3 board until someone gets three in a row or the board is full.

Requirements

- Python 3

How to run

From this folder:

    python tictac.py

How to play

1. X goes first.
2. On your turn, type a square number from 1 to 9.
3. Empty squares show their numbers. Taken squares show X or O.
4. The game ends when a player gets three in a row, or when every square is filled (a draw).
5. Type y to play again, or anything else to quit.

The board is numbered like this:

 1 | 2 | 3
---+---+---
 4 | 5 | 6
---+---+---
 7 | 8 | 9
